数据不上服务器。你的记录只存在你自己的浏览器里。看到学伴的进度，是因为他把「进度码」发给了你，不是因为网站在同步。所以每次想更新，就重新发一次码。

Nothing is uploaded to a server. Your records live only in your own browser. You can see your partner's progress because they sent you a progress code, not because the site syncs. To refresh it, they send a new code.
