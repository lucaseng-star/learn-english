禁用清单Do-not-say listรายการห้ามพูด

这几句语法都对，但在上面三个场合会直接扣分。看到自己想说，先停一秒换成右边。 All of these are grammatically fine, and all of them cost you points in the three situations above. When you catch yourself starting one, swap to the right-hand version. ทุกประโยคนี้ไวยากรณ์ถูกหมด แต่ในสามสถานการณ์ข้างบนมันทำให้เสียแต้ม ถ้าจับได้ว่ากำลังจะพูด ให้เปลี่ยนไปใช้ฝั่งขวา

别说Don't sayอย่าพูด 改说Say insteadพูดแบบนี้แทน
This is too expensive.That's a bit more than we'd budgeted for.
You're wrong. / It's wrong.That's not quite it yet.
We can't do that.I don't think we could do that.
You must do it like this.You might want to try it like this.
I want a discount.I was hoping there'd be some room on price.
No.Let me check and come back to you.
